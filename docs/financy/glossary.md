# Finance glossary

The finance words Financy's screens use, one term per thing in each of the nine languages. It
adds to the glossary in [i18n/TRANSLATING.md](../../i18n/TRANSLATING.md), which keeps the words
every Seed product shares (group, owner, archive, trash and the rest); a translation of a finance
screen uses both. A later issue that adds a finance word adds its row here first.

"Account" means two things in Financy. A person signs in to their **account** (TRANSLATING.md's
row); a household keeps its money in **finance accounts**, the word below. The two never share a
word where the language has a pair: ru аккаунт and счёт, zh-CN 账号 and 账户, ja アカウント and
口座. The English screens say just "account" for both, because each screen shows only one.
